On 21 November 2026 I attended [BSides Kathmandu](https://bsidesktm.com), the first BSides security conference in Nepal. I'm really grateful I got the chance to be there.

## What it is

BSides Kathmandu is a one-day, community-driven event. The idea is simple: technical talks, hands-on villages, a live Capture the Flag competition, and a stage reserved for first-time speakers (the Rookie Stage). It was great to see something like this finally happen in Nepal.

## How I got in

I had been doing a lot of CVE challenges on [CVEPlayground](https://cveplayground.com) and ended up at the top of the leaderboard. The CVEPlayground team sponsored my ticket for the conference, so a big thank you to them. If you want to practice with real CVEs, go and check it out.

## The talks and villages

The conference had a wide mix of talks. The parts that interested me most were the Rookie Stage and the hardware hacking village. I couldn't catch every talk, because I spent a good part of the day playing the CTF with my friends [Iconic](https://bijayakumal.com.np) and Kamisama.

I did get some time at the hardware hacking village, and it was a lot of fun to try something outside my usual web stuff.

## The CTF

We finished as the 3rd runner-up which was organized by the [Hack@sec Team](https://hackasec.com). We had a really great time playing together, and it was a good way to meet other people from the community.

## Final thoughts

I'd love to go again next year. Thanks to the organizers, the sponsors, and everyone who made the first BSides in Nepal happen.
